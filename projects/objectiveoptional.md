## Context
I love video games. I have loved them since I was very young. It sounds cliche, but I was a minecraft kid. I would play that game and the possibility would thrill me. I was and also still am an avid user of Youtube, and I would find series about modded minecraft, a novel concept in 2011, and it tickled my brain in a way I had never experienced before. I began to dream of ways that I would make changes to the game if I could. I had never considered making changes to a game itself, although one of my absolute favorite games at the time was Spore: Galatic Adventures, where you could create your own Sci-Fi quests within the context of the classic Spore universe. I spent hours of naivite trying to make something great. before I got into Dungeons and Dragons or Magic the gathering, before I delved into the wild west of software, that was what I did. Over the years, I learned that in order to truly create what I desired, I needed to learn how to program. Through an excellent fundamentals course and learning that my two year college had four year programs, I graduated with a Bachelors in Software Engineering.  Finally! Video game making time, right? Well... it is a lot harder than I thought. Its a lot harder than you probably think. It involves SO much more than just being able to program. Thankfully, so does Software Engineering. After dabbling for a long time at making small prototypes and following a few episodes down tutorial rabbit holes, an opportunity arose that would allow me to finally say what I have wanted to say for a long time: I AM a game developer.
